= FICTIONAL SIMULATION — Working product fact sheet

Northstar Mobility is preparing a six-month demand-responsive community
transport pilot in the fictional borough of Bellweather. Eligible residents
will be able to request journeys within a defined pilot area through a booking
service. Proposed operating hours, start date, eligibility, vehicle
specification and partner responsibilities remain subject to the final service
and partner records.

This fact sheet is a working source, not an approval to publish every statement.
Request substantiation for material claims and record unresolved uncertainty.
