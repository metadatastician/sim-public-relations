= Roadmap

. Validate the entry-level construct with career starters, career changers,
  hiring managers, agency and in-house practitioners, and educators.
. Obtain independent ethics, accessibility and safeguarding review of the
  Northstar Mobility scenario and every scored interpretation.
. Extend the native replay kernel into a local-first learner vertical slice:
  inbox, evidence requests, workbench, approvals, stakeholder responses,
  debrief, persistence and export.
. Add adversarial tests proving that hidden reality and model answers cannot
  enter learner bundles or AI prompts.
. Pilot paired human/AI and human-only runs to validate AI-direction and
  verification observations without rewarding tool access.
. Add a mentor/facilitator review workflow and calibration materials.
. Validate the evidence passport with employers without representing it as
  employment or accredited experience.
. Author progressive scenarios for agency, in-house, public-sector, charity,
  internal communications, public affairs and crisis contexts.
. Establish reviewed, effective-dated UK professional-practice reference packs;
  support other jurisdictions only through separately reviewed packs.
. Pursue qualification, CPD or recruitment partnerships only under separately
  named external approval and evaluation.
