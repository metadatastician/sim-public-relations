= Northstar Mobility — Authored Scenario Design
:toc: left

scenario_id: northstar-mobility-001

----

Everything in the scenario is fictional, including the organisation, agency,
people, publications, community groups, local authority, messages and data.

== Career-development purpose

The scenario supplies the experience usually hidden behind “one year in an
agency”: receive a brief, organise work, research context, maintain contact and
stakeholder records, produce drafts, manage feedback and approvals, respond to
an enquiry, monitor response, report against objectives and learn from a
changing situation.

Routine production matters, but it is embedded in judgement. A polished release
cannot compensate for an untested claim, false endorsement, irrelevant pitch,
missing approval or inaccessible information.

== Premise

Beacon & Rowe, a fictional small agency, is preparing the public launch of
Northstar Mobility's community minibus-booking pilot in the fictional borough
of Bellweather. Northstar describes the service as accessible and community-
designed. The initial brief seeks local coverage, partner amplification and
registrations on a modest budget within two working days.

The initial pack contains a client email, rough brief, product fact sheet,
stakeholder spreadsheet, draft executive quote and prior monitoring extract.
It contains gaps and ambiguous claims but does not label their correct
interpretation.

== Workstreams

Brief and account hygiene::
Clarify purpose, scope, role, deliverables, decisions, deadlines, budget,
confidentiality, AI policy and approval path.

Research and listening::
Build a source log; review the fictional transport context, prior commitments,
community concerns and accessible-communications needs; request missing
research proportionately.

Stakeholders and contacts::
Distinguish affected stakeholders from target audiences, repair the supplied
records, prioritise relevant fictional contacts and record relationship context.

Objectives, plan and measurement::
Translate business desires into communication objectives, baseline, activities,
outputs, out-takes and outcomes; state what cannot be attributed.

Content and channels::
Draft and revise a release, pitch, web copy, social copy, internal briefing,
Q&A, accessible summary and holding material. Maintain one factual spine while
adapting form.

Approvals and delivery::
Route claims and assets to the right fictional approvers, record changes,
communicate blockers and decide what can safely proceed.

Enquiry and issue response::
Handle a journalist's evidence-based question and an affected stakeholder's
accessibility concern; listen, verify, escalate and update recommendations.

Evaluation and reflection::
Classify fictional results honestly, correct duplicates or weak sentiment
inference, report against objectives and reflect on the decision path.

== AI task policy

The baseline run permits AI for ideation and variants using only disclosed,
non-personal scenario material. It prohibits entering the raw contact record,
unreleased personal statements or hidden facilitator material. Material use is
declared and every factual assertion is checked against disclosed sources.

An alternate `AI-review` run provides a plausible automated draft containing a
fabricated local statistic, an invented endorsement and inaccessible image
copy. The learner's task is to find, explain and correct the defects.

== Stable reality and variable consequences

Historical scenario facts never change with the learner's choice. Consequences
vary through logical time, evidence requests, stakeholder listening, approvals,
contact relevance, factual accuracy, accessibility, response handling and the
decision to launch, narrow, pause or reschedule activity.

== Defensible routes

`proceed-with-corrections`::
Narrow unsupported wording, obtain the required approvals, publish the verified
core material and schedule unresolved work separately.

`stage-the-launch`::
Proceed with low-risk owned/internal preparation while holding earned outreach
until claims and stakeholder questions are resolved.

`pause-and-replan`::
Recommend a short pause where the unresolved claim or accessibility issue is
material, communicate the reason and preserve a revised path to launch.

No route is automatically correct. Timing, disclosed evidence, mitigation and
quality of counsel determine defensibility.

== Assessment hooks

The scenario rewards relevant questions, source-linked claims, honest
uncertainty, specific objectives, meaningful measures, accessible work,
disciplined approval, relevant contact handling, correction and respectful
stakeholder response. It does not reward maximal output volume, flattering the
client, blanket distribution, manufactured enthusiasm or confident invented
detail.

== Content and learner safety

No task contacts a real person or publishes to a live service. Content is
watermarked. The fictional accessibility challenge is reviewed with disabled
practitioners and users before release; it must not use affected people merely
as a moral test for the learner. Time-pressure accommodations preserve the
ordering and prioritisation construct without penalising access needs.
