= Learning Model
:toc: left

== Construct

The assessed construct is defensible communications practice under ambiguity,
time pressure and organisational constraint. The record preserves what the
learner could reasonably know when acting, rather than judging only with
hindsight.

== Experience is repeated situated performance

The simulator does not claim that reading guidance or producing a pristine
press release equals experience. A learner encounters partial briefs, waiting
times, dependencies, corrections, conflicting preferences, ethical pressure,
unresponsive colleagues and stakeholder reaction. They must perform, observe
the consequence, receive debrief, retry and later transfer the capability to a
different context.

== Deliberate-practice cycle

. Orient: identify purpose, role, authority, deadlines and missing context.
. Investigate: gather and test relevant evidence without exhaustive research.
. Frame: distinguish facts, interpretations, assumptions and uncertainties.
. Plan: connect organisational purpose to audiences, objectives, strategy,
  activities and measures.
. Produce and coordinate: create fit-for-purpose artefacts, seek approval and
  maintain version and decision records.
. Relate: listen and respond to colleagues, clients, journalists and affected
  stakeholders without treating people as distribution channels.
. Monitor and adapt: interpret outputs, out-takes, outcomes and risk signals;
  change course when warranted.
. Reflect and transfer: explain the decision, recognise limitations and state
  how the approach changes in another situation.

== Feedback timing

`guided-practice` may offer prompts, examples and reversible rehearsal.
`assessment` provides process and accessibility affordances but no correctness
feedback before final submission. `mentor-review` permits a human to annotate
events and artefacts without rewriting the historical record.

== Route plurality

Rubrics describe competent standard routes, competent alternatives, minimally
acceptable work, common errors and critical errors. They never encode one
secret sequence, tone or vocabulary. A route is judged against the evidence,
authority and deadline at the time.

== AI contrast runs

Where useful, the same bounded task can be performed in three conditions:

* unaided, to reveal the learner's baseline reasoning and craft;
* AI-assisted, to observe instruction, selection, verification and improvement;
* AI-review, to test whether the learner can find planted defects in plausible
  automated output.

Tool speed and prose fluency are not competencies by themselves. Assessment
focuses on the quality and accountability of the professional act.
