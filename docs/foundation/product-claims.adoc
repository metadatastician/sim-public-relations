= Product Claims
:toc: left

== Permitted claims

* Fictional, source-attributed professional-learning simulator.
* Local-first practice in realistic public-relations and communications tasks.
* Evidence-linked observations of behaviour within a named scenario version.
* Watermarked portfolio of simulated work, decisions and reflection.
* Provisional professional-reference pack with an explicit review state and
  access date.

== Prohibited claims

The project and its outputs must not claim employment, client service,
placement or internship hours, supervised professional experience, guaranteed
competence, job readiness, guaranteed employment, professional membership,
qualification, accreditation or CPD unless a named competent external body
separately grants the relevant status.

No output may imply that a fictional stakeholder endorsed the learner or that
an automated score is an employer's hiring decision.

== Required export wording

Every portfolio document carries:

----
FICTIONAL SIMULATION — NOT LIVE CLIENT WORK OR EMPLOYMENT EXPERIENCE
----

The export identifies scenario, version, support level, AI condition, retries,
assessment status and review provenance.
