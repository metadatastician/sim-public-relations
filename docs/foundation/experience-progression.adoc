= Experience Progression
:toc: left

== Purpose

This progression breaks the hiring paradox into observable practice without
pretending a simulation is employment. Learners accumulate evidence across
varied scenarios and decreasing support.

[cols="1,2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Stage |Environment |Typical work |Evidence of readiness

|Observe and repair
|Worked examples; planted defects; generous time.
|Check claims, clean contact data, repair a brief, improve accessibility,
identify misleading metrics and find approval/version errors.
|Explains the defect, risk and correction rather than merely matching a model.

|Supported production
|Clear bounded tasks; checklists; mentor prompts.
|Research notes, monitoring summaries, media/stakeholder records, first drafts,
channel variants, proofing and asset/version control.
|Accurate, sourced, audience-fit work; declares uncertainty and AI use.

|Coordinated delivery
|Concurrent tasks; incomplete brief; dependencies and deadlines.
|Clarify scope, schedule work, request inputs, manage approval, maintain status,
handle a routine enquiry and report outputs.
|Prioritises, communicates blockers and leaves a usable audit trail.

|Bounded recommendation
|Competing options; limited budget; ethical and reputational risk.
|Audience and issue analysis, measurable objectives, channel and message
options, risk treatment, measurement plan and escalation.
|Recommendation follows evidence; alternatives and limitations are explicit.

|Integrated response
|Changing facts and stakeholder reaction; sparse support.
|Reframe, pause or redirect activity; correct the record; advise leaders;
coordinate responses; evaluate and reflect.
|Maintains accuracy, relationships and accountability while adapting quickly.
|===

== Breadth requirements

Progression requires evidence across contexts, not repeated mastery of one
launch template. A credible passport samples agency and in-house dynamics,
proactive and reactive work, internal and external audiences, routine and
high-risk tasks, owned/earned/shared/paid choices, measurement and an ethical
challenge. Each claimed capability needs more than one observation and at least
one transfer task with reduced scaffolding.

== Human review

Automated observations may point to event evidence and deterministic defects.
Claims about nuance, relationship quality, strategic judgement or professional
readiness require calibrated human review. Reviewers see the scenario version,
support level, retries, AI condition and accommodation mode.

== Employer-facing language

Permitted: `completed a fictional agency simulation`, `produced the attached
artefact under the recorded scenario constraints`, `received the following
evidence-linked observations`.

Prohibited: `worked for Northstar`, `managed a live launch`, `served a client`,
`has six months' experience`, or any claim of employment, accreditation or
professional status not separately earned.
