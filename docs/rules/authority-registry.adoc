== Interpretation controls

. Store a source URL, access date, effective/review date where known, quoted
  clause locator and local interpretation identifier.
. Do not score directly from a source until a competent reviewer approves the
  interpretation and its scenario application.
. Preserve historical packs for replay; do not silently apply current guidance
  to an old run.
. A missing, incompatible, revoked or mandatory-unreviewed pack invalidates any
  claim of authoritative assessment.
. Professional codes bind their members under their own terms. The simulator
  teaches relevant reasoning but does not claim regulatory jurisdiction over
  learners.
